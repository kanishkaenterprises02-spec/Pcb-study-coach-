Example — Physics

A body starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. Find final velocity.

Given:
u = 0 m/s
a = 2 m/s²
t = 5 s

Formula:
v = u + at

Substitution:
v = 0 + (2 × 5)

Answer:
v = 10 m/s
